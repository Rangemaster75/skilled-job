What matters most to you?

Choose a profile or drag the sliders. The ranking re-sorts as you go. Every trade is scored 1 to 5 on 14 factors, and 7 of those scores are judgment calls, marked J, so read them as a starting point to question.

Scores run 1 to 5, and 5 is always better for you. That means better paid, more in demand, cheaper to start, safer from AI, easier on the body.

Darker blue is a higher score. D marks published data, R marks data plus a rule I applied, J marks my judgment. Judgment cells have a dashed edge.

      How each column is scored

      Every factor is scored 1 to 5 for each trade, and 5 is always the better end. Where a published number exists, a fixed rule turns it into the score. Where none exists, the score is my judgment, and the card says so. The last line of each card says how far to trust it.

      Data a published figure, banded by a fixed rule Data + rule published figures plus adjustments I applied Judgment my reading of the evidence, no direct figure

      What the scores leave out

      Wages are Job Bank base hourly pay for fully certified workers (published November 2025), not annual income, overtime or union total packages. Outlooks are Job Bank's 2025–27 forecasts for Ontario and for the Windsor–Sarnia region, which includes Chatham-Kent. They are broad forecasts and the regional samples are small. Benefits count in both Job Stability and Benefits coverage, so set one of those sliders to zero if you do not want them counted twice.

      Check current wages, intake dates and apprenticeship sponsors with Skilled Trades Ontario, Job Bank and local employers before deciding. This is a comparison aid and not career advice.
